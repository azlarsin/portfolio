Method and boundary

  • Samples use SVG path length APIs, then explicit shape transforms place points in world coordinates.
  • Sampled minimum distance is guarded by segment intersections and point-in-polygon containment; overlap is invalid, not a positive gap.
  • Angle refinement first rejects overlap and target-gap deficits, then prefers a smaller sum of sampled gaps between adjacent indexed shapes; it is not a global packing objective.
